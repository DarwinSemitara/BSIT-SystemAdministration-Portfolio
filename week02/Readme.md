DRWN Technologies – Enterprise Infrastructure Planning
Project Overview

This project is part of ITEP 414 – System Administration and Maintenance, Week 2 at Laguna State Polytechnic University.

For this activity, I assumed the role of a Junior System Administrator for DRWN Technologies, a fictional startup software development company with 20 employees. The objective was to create a complete IT infrastructure plan before the company purchases and deploys its technology resources.

The project covers hardware, software, networking, system administration roles, security, backup strategies, and future infrastructure expansion.

Company Scenario

DRWN Technologies is a newly established software development company operating from a single office floor. The company has 20 employees distributed across four departments:

Department	Employees
Information Technology	5
Human Resources	4
Finance	5
Sales	6
Total	20

The company initially has no computers, servers, network infrastructure, internet setup, or formal security policies. This project provides a proposed infrastructure design to support the company's daily operations and future growth.

Challenges Encountered

One of the main challenges was determining the appropriate hardware and network equipment for a 20-person startup while keeping the proposed infrastructure practical and scalable. Designing the network topology also required considering security, connectivity, resource sharing, and future expansion.

Reflection

This activity helped me understand that system administration involves more than installing computers and configuring networks. Proper planning is important because infrastructure decisions directly affect an organization's productivity, security, reliability, and ability to grow.

The project also gave me practical experience in documenting technical decisions and designing an infrastructure based on business requirements. These skills will help me become more organized and prepared for future system administration tasks.
